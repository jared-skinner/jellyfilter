Playback filtering

The server watches each playing session and seeks past or mutes filtered scenes. This works on every client and is the only mechanism that can tell users apart.
How often playback positions are checked. Lower values start the skip closer to the beginning of a scene at the cost of more polling.
How far past the end of a scene playback resumes. A little padding stops a slow client from landing back inside the scene it just left.
Scenes shorter than this are left alone, because seeking would be more disruptive than the content.

Filter files

One pattern per line, searched in order next to the video file. {name} becomes the video filename without its extension and {filename} becomes the full filename.
Optional. Searched after the video's own folder, for libraries mounted read-only.

Media segments

Lets clients that understand media segments offer their own skip button. Segments belong to an item rather than a viewer, so every scene published this way is visible to everyone on the server regardless of their preferences below.
Jellyfin's segment vocabulary has no entry for content warnings, so scenes have to borrow one of the existing types.

Per-user filtering

Each user chooses which categories to filter. A category left at Off plays untouched. Mute silences the client for the length of the scene instead of seeking past it.
Use the same name that appears in the type field of your filter files.

Troubleshooting
